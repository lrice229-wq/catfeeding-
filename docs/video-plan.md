# Lucky Paws: colony stories

Use the real uploaded colony footage. These are proposed edits, not completed videos. Review the clips before selecting shots; the plan below does not assume every shot is already available.

## First film: They know someone is coming

Length: 30–45 seconds. Start with natural sound. Keep captions readable on phones. Make a portrait version for social sharing and a landscape version for the website.

| Time | Proposed picture | Proposed voiceover / caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | A cat watching or approaching, filmed at its level | “They know someone is coming.” |
| 5–12s | The caretaker arriving and preparing food | “Every day, someone shows up.” |
| 12–22s | Food being served, fresh water, cats settling to eat | “With food. With water. With care.” |
| 22–32s | A quiet close-up and the caretaker's hands | “Lucky Paws Foundation supports community cats and the people who care for them in St. Thomas.” |
| 32–45s | A calm final moment, followed by a clear end card | “Help the care continue. Support colony care through HSSTT.” |

End card: Lucky Paws Foundation · 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Give through HSSTT. Link to https://humanesocietystthomas.org/programs/tnvr in the post and website button.

## Follow-up films

- **Meet one of the cats:** one cat's name, personality, and true story, told by the person who knows them. Avoid inventing a rescue history.
- **The person behind the bowls:** a caretaker explains why they keep returning and what help would mean.
- **After the return:** connect TNVR with ongoing food, water, and colony care. Use HSSTT's program as the information source.

## Footage to capture on a future feeding round

Record steady 8–12 second shots: cats approaching, preparing food, filling water, eating, checking on individual cats, cleaning bowls, and a quiet moment after feeding. Record a short caretaker interview: “What do you wish people knew about these cats?” Keep some natural sound without music.

Avoid showing exact colony locations or identifiable people without permission. Use licensed music if added. Confirm names, current counts, and each cat's story with the caretaker before including them. Do not stage distress or imply a donation buys a specific quantity of care without verified costs.

## Sources and brand direction

The owner identifies Lucky Paws Foundation as a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. HSSTT's TNVR page names Lucky Paws Foundation as a colony-care partner and describes daily food and water support. Financial donations should be directed through HSSTT, rather than collected on this site.
