A simple idea, rooted here

Support the people already showing up.

Across St. Thomas, community members look after outdoor cats by offering food, water, and a familiar place to return to. Island Paws Pantry is a starting point for bringing neighbors, food donors, and local animal-welfare partners together around that everyday care.

This page is an early concept. The pantry’s organizers, donation process, and partner details have not been finalized. We’ll add verified information here before accepting contributions.

The local picture

What we know—and what still needs counting.

Good care starts with honest information. Here’s a grounded snapshot of animal-welfare work in the territory, without guessing at the number of cats on the streets.

1,100+

Veterinary services in St. Thomas

In June 2025, a two-week U.S. military and VI Department of Health partnership delivered more than 1,100 no-cost veterinary services valued at over $127,000 to residents on St. Thomas. This is a community veterinary-services figure—not a cat-colony count.

Source: VI Department of Health
No census

A reliable island-wide cat count

We haven’t found a current, publicly available census of community cats for St. Thomas. A verified count could help local groups understand needs, plan food support, and track humane spay-and-neuter work over time.

Local context: the Humane Society of St. Thomas TNVR program works with community cat caretakers.
A St. Thomas example from a 2022 guide. A community-cat guide posted in 2022 described Lucky Paws Foundation caring for more than 600 cats in over 75 colonies on St. Thomas at that time. Before you give

Questions, answered honestly.

We’re still getting the practical pieces in place. Here’s what visitors should know right now.

Early concept · Details pending
Can I donate money or food through this page today?

The store links lead to their online shops, but the pantry has not yet set up a payment service or a drop-off address. Please wait for verified instructions before sending donations.

Is Island Paws Pantry a registered nonprofit?

That has not been established. The project name and organizational structure are still being explored. No tax-deductibility claim is being made.

I care for a colony. Can I request food?

A food-support request process is being planned but is not open yet. Check back for an application or contact details once the pantry is ready.

What kind of food or supplies can I give?

We’ll post a list of the most useful food and supplies, along with donation and drop-off instructions, once collection is arranged.

How does this relate to TNVR?

TNVR means trap, neuter, vaccinate, and return. It is one approach used by animal-welfare organizations to care for community cats. This pantry concept focuses on food support and hopes to share information from established local partners.

Where will the pantry serve?

This pantry is being planned specifically for community-cat caretakers on St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands.
